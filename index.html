04 RESEARCH PIPELINE

Better questions, before better scores.

Four ranked hypotheses, not claims of improvement. Testing is stopped by the literal uniqueness rule and an unassigned method lane.

Full preregistration ↗
01HIGH EFFORT

Magnetic texture displacement

Look for a consistent offset of magnetic patterns across a gravity gradient, including under alluvium.

Confounder: volcanic or intrusive contacts.
02HIGH EFFORT

Fan-channel offset consistency

Test repeated channel displacement against corroborating magnetic structure in high-resolution terrain.

Confounder: channel avulsion and roads.
03HIGH EFFORT

Thermal residuals at transfer zones

Remove sampling-effort bias before relating geothermal anomalies to potential-field intersections.

Confounder: groundwater and selective sampling.
04MEDIUM EFFORT

Multi-scale edge polarity

Examine signed magnetic and gravity profiles, not just the strength of their gradients.

Confounder: non-fault lithologic contacts.
