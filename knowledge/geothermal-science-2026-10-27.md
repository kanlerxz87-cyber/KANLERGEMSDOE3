## The historical GEMSDOE32 family

The independently rerun [geometry receipt](../evidence/h33-lineage-20261027.json) shows that the pinned GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 positive-dot masks match exactly on the mirrored footprint. The pinned GEMSDOE32 B2 mask is identical to the GEMSDOE30 mask after removing 6,436 dots at Euclidean pixel-center distance at most two pixels from the mirrored known-fault catalogue; it adds zero dots. This is pixel equivalence, **not** proof of how the authors generated it. The user-reported historical 0.2778 has no organizer submission-page receipt linking that value to the saved bytes.

The [official metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric) gives each truth pixel its maximum distance-weighted prediction credit and penalizes uncredited prediction mass. Removing redundant mass may improve the tradeoff, but deleting a useful nearby prediction can reduce truth credit. The private labels and a paired ablation on common folds are absent; there is no defensible causal attribution or probability of exceeding the historic claim. At exactly three 100-m pixels the triangular score kernel is zero, whereas the requested uniqueness test counts dots **within or at** three pixels. These are deliberately different rules.

## Important data semantics

- The pinned participant-mirror `sample_submission.tif` equals the mirrored label values inside the footprint and contains positive catalogue pixels, whereas the official page describes an all-absence sample. We use it only as a scoped grid/mask reference; its organizer provenance is unverified. See [`sample-provenance-check.json`](../evidence/sample-provenance-check.json).
- The mirror labels band 15 `depth_to_base_surf` as basement depth. The organizer's feature list describes *depth to conductive base surface*, which need not mean geological basement depth. Do not substitute one physical interpretation for the other without source metadata.
- The mirror calls band 6 `tc` a magnetic tilt/curvature layer; the organizer page also shows a radiometric total-count visualization. The shared name by itself does not authenticate the band meaning. Obtain authoritative band metadata before using `tc` as either magnetic or radiometric evidence.
- Dilatation, shear magnitude and the second strain-rate invariant are scalar summaries; they do not by themselves determine principal strain orientation. A spatial gradient's orientation is a different quantity and must not be described as a recovered strain-tensor direction.
