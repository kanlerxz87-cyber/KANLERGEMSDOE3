## Frozen single lane and protocol

The prompt references a "single method paragraph below" but supplies no clearly assigned lane. We choose the narrow, provisional **bilateral K/Th texture displacement at a LiDAR scarp** lane for research only; absence of an externally assigned lane blocks promotion. No H19-5 or other prior predictions are inputs. A single fixed candidate, no hyperparameter search, maximum three experiments and two hours. Use the pinned shared GEMSDOE24 `SegmentHoldout`, its metric and `submission_writer.py`, and cached input layers; no private fork of the shared evaluator. Whole-component spatial splits, a 1.5 km training guard, fold-visible-only catalogue use, pixel-exact visible-fault exclusion, 300 m triangular kernel, alpha 0.2/beta 0.8, pooled components, and spatial-block 95% CI. Cached physical features do not depend on catalogue labels. Canary each source and transformed feature before trusting DTI; either-tail AUC >0.90 is a stop. Compare continuous surface before placement to the registry and the candidate dots after placement. Absolute Spearman >0.90 or directed 3-pixel overlap >70% against **any** registered raster is a duplicate stop, without density or calibration exceptions. Incomplete registry coverage is not a pass. No weekly slot will be selected or spent. A score cannot be inferred from a holdout without independent calibration to organizer receipts.

The official problem text requests null/NaN outside its bounds. Some users report portal range errors for NaN. The pinned shared writer defaults to finite zero, whereas the strict sample-mask validator rejects that outside encoding. A finite-zero alternative is a local compatibility test only, not proven official-compliant; do not assume it fixes every rejection. A central shared-template fix or organizer clarification is required.

### Fixed execution details (recorded before implementing this candidate)

The intended final reference budget is **37,654** binary dots, to match the historical H33-B2 count rather than tuning density on this holdout. This budget was recorded **after** discovering the lattice coverage obstruction; we did not reduce it to work around the rule. A priori geometry proves a strict stop is likely. The experiment is research only regardless of outcome.

For each pixel with valid cached LiDAR and positive cached K and Th, decode scarp `step_max` as `((q-1)/254)^2`, coherence as `(q-1)/254`, and strike as `(q-1)*pi/254` clockwise from north. Source convention is independently checked against [7GEMSDOE dem_merge.py at its data-generation commit](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/7GEMSDOE/blob/2e7bf080270493f77286fd62f2db958805053ba5/scripts/dem_merge.py#L88-L99). Sample each quantized radiometric band at five along-strike offsets `[-4,-2,0,2,4]`, at positions +/-3 pixels normal to strike. Compute centered normalized profile correlation at tangential lags -2, 0 and +2, with nearest-pixel sampling and full valid support. For each channel, improvement is max(nonzero-lag correlations) minus zero-lag correlation, clipped at zero; use it only if the nonzero winner is positive. The joint displacement feature is the geometric mean of the two positive improvements when their preferred nonzero lag has the same sign. This is an intentionally simple correlation heuristic, not proven slip direction or fault age. Inputs are quantized/derived: agreement in K and Th is not statistically independent evidence.

For the one experiment, fit two otherwise-identical histogram gradient boosting classifiers: candidate gets `[K, Th, step, coherence, sin(2*strike), cos(2*strike), K_gain, Th_gain, joint_gain]`; ablation gets the first six. Both use only labelled visible components in the fold's buffered training area and a deterministic sample of visible-only background; no catalogue-distance or registry features. The sample submission contributes its grid and footprint, never values as a feature. One fold at a time, emit the same area-proportional share of 37,654 dots by the existing template's 3-pixel priority Poisson, excluding visible catalogue pixels exactly. Canary all nine features, their support mask and a pre-placement candidate surface before trusting results. Pool 300-m triangular DTI terms in the shared `vendor/gems57/evaluate_holdout.py` and obtain paired 95% spatial-block confidence intervals. A candidate cannot be promoted on the ablation result alone: the strongest comparable holdout record, full registry, lane assignment, independent receipt, and a separate selector must also pass. The registry snapshot and budget do not establish coverage of concurrent or unregistered rasters.
