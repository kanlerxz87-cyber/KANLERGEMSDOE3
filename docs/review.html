What the evidence actually shows.

A verified geometric blocker, a nine-file historical comparison, and a clear distinction between a valid file and a validated discovery.

01 / Decision

Why the run stopped

A SHA-verified 17GEMSDOE prior is positive at every one of the 5,167,373 valid footprint pixels. The unchanged template treats every finite value >0 as support. Thus, any nonempty prediction supported on the footprint has 100% of dots within zero pixels of this witness, exceeding the required >70% stop. One witness is conclusive for rejection but is not a complete registry scan.

The independent spacing-5 lattice covers 5,160,724 footprint cells within 3 pixels; only 6,649 lie outside. More than 22,163 distinct dots necessarily violate the same 70% threshold against the lattice. Inspect the machine-readable certificate ↗

02 / Historical evidence

Related masks, not independent wins

All 72 directed comparisons among the nine reviewed historical masks exceed the threshold. GEMSDOE25 and 30 share a decoded footprint mask. GEMSDOE31 and 32 are successive 1- and 2-pixel catalogue-collar removals; 36 contains all 37,654 GEMSDOE32 dots plus 1,200. These facts describe geometry, not a cause of performance.

The 0.2778 GEMSDOE32 value is an unverified owner report. No file-bound submission receipt was identified, and lack of one does not prove no submission was scored. The public leaderboard showed 0.4437 on 26 October 2026. This is context, not an accepted file-bound result under the project receipt rule, and it may change.

03 / Trust boundaries

Four different evidence classes

  • REGISTRY-MEASUREMENT: reproducible geometry and support; not model performance.
  • HOLDOUT-DTI: pooled spatial evaluation with evaluator version, withheld positives and 95% confidence interval; not run here.
  • ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED: a submission-page receipt tied to the exact file; none identified here.
  • Owner report or projection: an unverified lead, not an established score.

Public owner-mirror hashes establish integrity, not official origin, licensing, absence of leakage or permission to train. The sample's positive cells reproduce the catalogue, so use it only for its grid and footprint, never as a predictor. A shared metric docstring claim was disproved by a synthetic example; the exact evaluator was not shown defective, and upstream issue creation was denied by the integration.
