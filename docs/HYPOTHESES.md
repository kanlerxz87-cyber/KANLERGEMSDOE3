## Experiment guardrails

- Work from the shared template and cached feature stack only; do not recalculate catalogue-derived features with withheld faults present.
- Hold out entire fault segments with a buffer; mask the visible positive pixels exactly; report pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and a 95% CI.
- Score every individual feature as a leakage canary before using its combination. AUC above 0.90 is presumed leakage until resolved.
- Check candidate score surface against the complete registry before placement and final-dot overlap after placement. Correlation >0.90 or >70% of dots within 3 px of any registry raster is duplicate-stop.
- Maximum three experiments or two hours. No selection or submission slot in this phase.
- A candidate not beating the matched current holdout best cannot be promoted, even if its narrative is plausible or its raw score surface looks geologically attractive.
