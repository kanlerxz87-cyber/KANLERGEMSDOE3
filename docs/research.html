Full hypotheses & caveats ↗

01 / Moderate CPU

Magnetic–gravity interface under cover

Test multi-scale edge persistence and axial alignment with cover-dependent gating. A lithologic contact or survey striping can mimic the signal.

02 / Moderate CPU

Topographic step with gravity support

Compare step profiles at multiple scales with an independent subsurface gradient. Terraces, shorelines and rock contacts are plausible mimics.

03 / Low–moderate CPU

Strain transition at a magnetic contact

Use coarse geodetic strain as a contextual prior, not a fault label. GPS interpolation and geological contacts can cause similar patterns.

04 / Deferred

Shallow thermal-gradient corroboration

GDR lists public probe data, but download, coverage and field suitability were not verified here. Soil moisture and seasonal heating are mimics.

A mirror is not an official receipt.
