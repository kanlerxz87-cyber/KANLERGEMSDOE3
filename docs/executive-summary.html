Understanding the “[0, 1]” error

The exact cause of the prior error is unknown: we do not have its rejected bytes or portal receipt. Typical causes include NaN or infinity, a nodata sentinel, scores outside [0,1], or writing the wrong band. A CRS, shape or transform mismatch is a separate format failure.

The official description calls for null/NaN outside the training-data bounds and probabilities in [0,1]. Treating the irregular mask of the unauthenticated sample mirror as the exact boundary is a conservative local policy, not a verified portal rule. One pinned writer defaults to zero outside while another defaults to NaN. Reopen the saved TIFF, inspect its mask, raw values, band count, float32, CRS, dimensions and transform; do not silently clip or assume either encoding guarantees acceptance. No local check proves what caused the prior portal error.
