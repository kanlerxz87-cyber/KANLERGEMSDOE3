Why GEMSDOE32 H33 B2 scored 0.2778

Verified line by line from the pinned files (receipt: scripts/focus9_audit.py).

  • H33 B2 has 37,654 dots. GEMSDOE30 (and GEMSDOE25, an identical mask) has 44,090.
  • Removing dots within 2 px of the known catalogue takes 30 → 32 exactly: 6,436 removed, 0 added. Equality test: H33 == GEMSDOE30 AND NOT(within 2 px of catalogue) is True.
  • Owner-reported values: GEMSDOE30 0.2600 → GEMSDOE32 0.2778 (+0.0178). This single paired comparison is the cleanest mechanism evidence we have, with n=1.
  • Masking: the catalogue mask is pixel-exact with no 300 m buffer, per the organizer masking clarification cited in Evidence (the thread itself was not re-fetched in this session).

Plausible mechanisms (not causal proof): (a) a lower FP mass at the same TP (the 0.2 weight on FP); (b) the freed dot budget lands elsewhere; (c) a random or different owner-side difference could also explain part of +0.0178. We cannot separate these with the receipts we have.

What the catalogue holdout says: the leaky holdout (withheld catalogue segments) ranks the nine focus files in the opposite order to the owner values (Spearman -0.85, n=9). We treat it as a leakage diagnostic and do not use it to select a file.

What the SGMC-CAL proxy says: ranking agreement with the owner values is Spearman 0.95 (p≈9e-05, n=9). This is the only proxy here that agrees with the owner values. It is weak evidence: n=9, owner-reported values, and no organizer receipt.

Irregularities and limits (flagged for review)

  1. Provenance. Every input is an owner mirror on GitHub, pinned by SHA-256. None is an organizer-authenticated download. The organizer sites were unreachable from this sandbox (drivendata.org), so no organizer page was read in this session.
  2. Competition rules. The competition rules for using participant mirrors of competition data have not been checked here. Confirm before any upload.
  3. Leaderboard values. 0.2778 and the other focus values are owner-reported. The user also cited 0.4413 (high) and 0.3195 (reference). None of these has an organizer receipt, and they have not been reconciled.
  4. Sample raster. The mirrored sample_submission.tif has finite values only inside its footprint, and its 60,988 positive pixels exactly equal the labelled positives. Our source register records that this contradicts the organizer's sample description. We used only its grid and footprint, never its values.
  5. Range error. The reported rejection ("Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]") has no uploaded bytes or organizer response to diagnose. One hypothesis is that NaN cells outside the footprint are read as out of range. This is unverified. We wrote the zeros variant, which is finite and in [0,1] across the full grid; it is quarantined and not offered for download. docs/SOURCES.md notes the organizer does not say whether zero-valued cells outside the mask are rejected, so portal acceptance is still unverified.
  6. Uniqueness scope. The inventory covers the repositories listed in the request, at their default-branch HEAD only. Earlier commits and repositories not in the list are out of scope. Inventory counts are in the receipts linked below.
  7. Uniqueness judgment. The 70% rule does not say which side of the overlap it measures. We read it two-ways, which is the conservative reading. Under the candidate-side reading alone, this file would pass (its own dots are 5.5% inside GEMSDOE69's set). That reading is recorded in the receipt but is not the verdict.
  8. Near-threshold files. Other GEMSDOE69 submissions (39,958 dots) reach 68.7% peer-side coverage and GEMSDOE37 `rankmix-strain-topo` reaches 66.3%; both sit below the 70% stop. They are listed in the receipt under near_threshold_files.
  9. Excluded inputs. 152 inventoried rasters are not submission-format (dense masks above 200,000 dots, integer or out-of-[0,1] feature maps) and were not compared. 34 are on a different grid and 1 is unreadable. These exclusions are visible in the receipt, not hidden.
  10. Proxy validity. The SGMC-CAL proxy and the catalogue holdout are not organizer scores. Their agreement with the leaderboard rests on n=9 owner values.
  11. Shared evaluator. The pinned evaluator fails a synthetic empty-truth operator check (GEMSDOE32 issue #41, cited and not re-checked here). We used it unmodified for measurement only.
