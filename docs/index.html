03 / Interpretation

Why GEMSDOE32 stood out.

What we can verify

In the pinned owner mirrors, H33-B2 is exactly the 44,090-dot D2.8 mask minus the 6,436 dots at or within two pixels of the mapped catalogue, leaving 37,654. Its 0.2778 is an unverified owner report, not a receipt.

What we cannot infer

Removing predictions can reduce false-positive mass, but can also lose valuable fault coverage. Hidden labels and causal ablation are unavailable, so thinning is a hypothesis, not proof of why a score changed. Whether any new method can surpass it is unknown.
