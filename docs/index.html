01

Cross-scale edge phase disagreement

UntestedMedium cost

Core TMI/RTP; upward-continued TMI at 150 m; GeoDAWN K/Th/U or ratio grids.

Persistent magnetic edges with signed edge-normal radiometric derivative reversal or termination, rather than coincident large gradient magnitudes.

Confounder: Intrusive lithologic contacts or flight-block levelling seams.

02

Repeated drainage displacement graph

UntestedHigh cost

Core detrended elevation (band 12), elevation slope (band 19), derived drainage graph; optional 3DEP high-resolution DEM.

Repeated signed lateral offsets across independent tributaries, with graph-level agreement across scales instead of a single scarp maximum.

Confounder: Stream capture, fan avulsion or abandoned lake shorelines.

03

Alteration-front termination pairs

UntestedMedium cost

GeoDAWN K/Th/U or Th/K and U/K; independent magnetic edge or analytic-signal axes.

Pair opposite-side along-strike terminations of alteration fronts across a magnetic edge; do not select merely high-ratio pixels.

Confounder: Erosional unconformities, lithologic boundaries or soil-moisture effects.

04

Terrain-conditioned thermal sign asymmetry

UntestedHigh cost

GDR #1391 well/spring temperature and sampling metadata; elevation, aspect and drainage position.

Robust terrain-corrected temperature residuals with repeated cross-line sign consistency, rather than nearest-hot-point distance.

Confounder: Groundwater advection, seasonality, pumping or land-use effects.
