03 / FINDINGS
01

Pruning, not a new detector.

The H33-B2 raster is exactly the D2.8 mask after a two-pixel catalogue-distance exclusion. Spacing and pruning could improve metric efficiency; the hidden truth is needed to prove why.

Read the forensic review ↗
02

The literal rule constrains every dot.

The registered lattice is within three pixels of 99.87% of the footprint. At the usual 37,654-dot budget, the literal 70% overlap threshold is mathematically unreachable against that lattice.

Inspect the byte-level audit ↗
03

Holdout is not the leaderboard.

Catalogued-fault recovery is a useful, biased proxy for withheld new faults. Compare only matched folds and evaluators; never present a projection as an organizer score.
