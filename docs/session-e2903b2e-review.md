## Why the reported H33 B2 result may be relatively high

Under the published distance-tolerant Tversky structure, a truth cell receives its strongest nearby weighted prediction credit, while uncredited emitted mass contributes false-positive burden. Redundant nearby dots need not add truth credit. Therefore pruning an H19-derived emission can plausibly improve precision while preserving most 300 m coverage. The observed B2 operation removes a catalogue-adjacent annulus without adding new detections, which is consistent with that mechanism.

It does **not** prove the mechanism caused 0.2778. The score is owner-reported without a submission-page receipt bound to these bytes; no paired fold-local ablation isolates the deletion; hidden truth is unavailable; and the shared local evaluator is defective. The project cannot defensibly estimate whether a new file will exceed 0.2778, 0.3195, 0.4413 or the separately dated public-page observation.
