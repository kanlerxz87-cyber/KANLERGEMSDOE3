Why GEMSDOE32 reported 0.2778 (what is verified, what is not)

Verified geometry (pinned audit receipt, not a score): the GEMSDOE32 H33-2-B2 dot set is the GEMSDOE25/30 44,090-dot mask with 6,436 dots removed and 0 added. Every removed dot lies within 2 px (Euclidean) of the mirrored catalogue; none lies beyond it. Source: h33-lineage-20261027.json.

Mechanism (plausible, not proven): under the shared metric, catalogue pixels are excluded from evaluation, so a dot on a known fault earns no credit and is not penalised. A dot beside a known fault can only score if the hidden truth continues there. Pruning catalogue-flank dots frees budget for dots elsewhere. This explains why a subset can score differently; it does not show the owner’s +0.0178 gain, because no organizer receipt is available.

Our same-method test of this rule (E2, holdout): pooled HOLDOUT-DTI 0.1118 with flank exclusion versus 0.1146 without (E1). In our holdout the rule does not help. Our holdout withholds whole catalogue segments as the hidden truth, which may not match the official hidden labels. This mismatch is an open irregularity.

Irregularities: the owner's own audit note says “UNSCORED” and projects 0.2747, not 0.2778. The 0.2778 value is therefore an owner claim. A projection is never written here as a score.
