# CPX archive: what the reported H33 result does and does not establish

**DO NOT SUBMIT.** This corrects an earlier CPX presentation that suggested a
selector could adopt a detector-only registry scope and that hidden truth credit
could be inferred from unverified historical score claims. The original prose
is preserved at
[`archive/integration-cfcca804-cpx/research__cpx_why_02778_20261028.md.snapshot`](../archive/integration-cfcca804-cpx/research__cpx_why_02778_20261028.md.snapshot)
for history, not as a submission guide.

The user-reported GEMSDOE32 value **0.2778** has no organizer submission-page
receipt tied to the referenced TIFF. The pinned [H33 geometry audit](../evidence/h33-lineage-20261027.json)
shows that GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 share the same 44,090-dot mask, and the
37,654-dot GEMSDOE32 mask removes 6,436 dots within two pixels of the mirrored
known-fault catalogue without adding dots. That is geometry, not proof of hidden
truth, attribution, model ancestry or the cause of a claimed score.

The [organizer's metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric)
can reward nearby true-fault coverage while penalizing prediction mass, so
removing redundant or poorly placed dots is a plausible explanation; it can also
remove true coverage. [Organizer staff](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4)
state that the known-fault mask is pixel-exact. A 200 m dead zone, the lost
true-positive credit, and a causal increase to 0.2778 are **not established**.
Without hidden labels, matching score receipts and a paired ablation, no reliable
new-file leaderboard estimate or guarantee of improvement is available.

The [independent CPX saved-byte audit](../evidence/session-cfcca804-cpx-audit.json)
finds **37,648 / 37,654 (99.98%)** of its dots within 3 pixels of the full
registered binary lattice. Its earlier detector-only comparisons cannot override
this literal duplicate stop, and a selector cannot waive it. The audit does not
rerun the historical holdout, authenticate the participant-mirror template, or
establish portal acceptance. The zero-outside TIFF passes `finite-grid` but fails
`template-mask` and `masked-finite` locally; the user's rejected file was not
available to diagnose the previous range error.
