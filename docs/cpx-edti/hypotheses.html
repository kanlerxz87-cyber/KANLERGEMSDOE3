#3 H-CPX-3 — Conductive-basement step coincident with magnetic source-depth transition

queued Cost: Medium-high (step pairing + persistence tracing) · External data: None

Layers: depth_to_base_surf, cond_surf, tmi vertical/horizontal gradients, rtp
Signature: Paired step edges: lateral gradient ridge of basement depth/conductivity within 1 px of a magnetic gradient ridge, sustained over >=1 km along strike
Why off-catalogue: A faulted basement contact juxtaposes conductive basin fill against resistive basement and offsets magnetic sources; the paired step can be traced under cover far from mapped surface breaks
Difference: Conductivity/basement used as point features before; an explicit two-field paired-step coincidence with along-strike persistence is new in this repo
Non-fault mimic: Non-faulted basement lithologic contacts; basin-margin onlap; inversion artefacts in the depth surface
