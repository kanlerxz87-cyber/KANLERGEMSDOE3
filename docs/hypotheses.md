## Ranked hypotheses

### 1. Windowed potential-field spectral-slope break — medium cost; planning hypothesis only

- **Layers:** `rtp`, `tmi`, `iso_grav_anom`; analyze each field separately before any fusion.
- **Signature:** in fixed, preregistered windows, estimate radial log-power versus wavenumber slopes at fixed wavelength bands. Test for a co-located change in long- versus short-wavelength decay across a potential-field boundary. Do not use catalogue distance or known-fault locations as features.
- **Why it may find an unmapped fault:** a buried basement fault may juxtapose source-depth or magnetic/density domains under alluvium, where no crisp scarp or single-scale edge is visible.
- **Difference from this repository:** the local and current peer work already includes Fourier upward continuation, Euler/source-depth, local-wavenumber and multiscale edge methods. The focused scan found no exact windowed radial spectral-slope change-point implementation. This is a narrow distinction, not a global novelty claim.
- **Non-fault mimic:** intrusive/lithologic contacts, basin-margin source mixing, line-levelling seams, regional trends and window leakage.
- **Expected DTI improvement:** qualitatively plausible small-to-moderate positive `HOLDOUT-DTI` increment if repeatable; zero or negative is equally plausible. **No numeric estimate.**
- **Data availability:** uses existing participant-mirror bands only; no new official data is needed for the concept. It is nevertheless **not executable** until a lane is assigned, the central cache is supplied, and the evaluator and registry gates are repaired.

### 2. Fault-offset geometry of non-fault geologic contacts — high cost; not currently viable

- **Layers:** official bedrock/geologic-map unit polygons and *non-fault* contact boundaries, with RTP/gravity only as independent corroboration. Explicitly exclude fault linework and pin scale, edition, metadata, and license.
- **Signature:** repeated, compatible truncation or offset of multiple lithologic contacts across a narrow corridor, aligned with an independent potential-field edge.
- **Why it may find an unmapped fault:** a concealed structure can displace or terminate mapped units even where the fault trace itself is absent.
- **Difference from this repository:** the prepared bands do not contain non-fault contact geometry. Existing work contains fault and potential-field proxies; this proposes map-unit displacement rather than a fault-map overlay.
- **Non-fault mimic:** unconformity, erosion, facies change, map-sheet join, scale mismatch, or georeferencing error.
- **Expected DTI improvement:** unknown and map-coverage-dependent; no numeric estimate. No suitable mapping means no expected gain.
- **Official source:** [USGS National Geologic Map Database](https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/). This is only a discovery index. Exact footprint coverage, scale, license/metadata, contact geometry, and obtainable bytes were **not verified here**, so this is deferred—not a viable recommendation.

### 3. Hydrothermal-alteration contrast across independent structure — high cost; not currently viable

- **Layers:** potentially new NASA/USGS LP DAAC ASTER surface-reflectance observations, with existing RTP/gravity edges as independent structure. Pin product/version, dates, cloud/vegetation mask, and usable coverage before designing features.
- **Signature:** a coherent mineral/alteration proxy changes across or terminates against a potential-field lineament; do not score an alteration anomaly by itself.
- **Why it may find an unmapped fault:** permeable faults can channel hydrothermal fluid and produce alteration where the surface trace is obscured.
- **Difference from this repository:** no ASTER alteration layer occurs in the 19 prepared bands or the searched local/current peer code. Geothermal, temperature, and geochemistry methods overlap in purpose, so only the proposed sensor layer is distinct; global method novelty is not established.
- **Non-fault mimic:** clay-rich sediment, mineralogy, playa/saline soil, vegetation, albedo, and atmospheric/acquisition artifacts; real faults may have no alteration signature.
- **Expected DTI improvement:** highly uncertain and indirect; likely zero outside exposed altered ground. **No numeric estimate.**
- **Official source:** [NASA/USGS LP DAAC product catalog](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/products/). Exact product, access requirements, AOI coverage, usable pixels, and downloadable bytes were **not checked or obtained**. This is deferred—not viable.
